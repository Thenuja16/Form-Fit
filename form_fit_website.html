01 / The idea

Training with feedback, not guesswork.

FORM FIT is designed to make exercise technique easier to understand while you train.

⌁

Track movement

A motion sensor measures changes in wrist and forearm movement during a bicep curl.

◉

Recognise technique

A trained machine-learning model can classify movement patterns as correct or incorrect, based on its training data.

↗

Give instant feedback

A vibration motor and small display can alert the user when the detected movement needs attention.

04 / Technique guide

Common curl mistakes.

These are general technique reminders. FORM FIT's actual alerts depend on the movements and labels used to train its model.

↗

1. Swinging the body

Using momentum can make the curl less controlled. Try a stable stance and a smooth movement.

↕

2. Moving too quickly

Fast, uncontrolled repetitions make it harder to maintain consistent technique. Slow down and stay in control.

⌁

3. Bending the wrist

Try to keep the wrist in a comfortable, neutral position instead of letting it fold sharply.

⇅

4. Incomplete movement

Use a comfortable range of motion that you can control. Do not force the joint to move farther than is comfortable.

◌

5. Elbow drifting

Keep the upper arm reasonably steady. Avoid letting the elbow travel around to create momentum.

!

6. Choosing too much weight

Choose a manageable load that allows controlled repetitions. Stop if you feel pain or unusual discomfort.

The technology behind FORM FIT.

A compact embedded system combines motion sensing, machine learning and user feedback.

How it works

From movement to feedback

Sense

The IMU captures motion data from the arm.

Process

The ESP32-S3 reads and prepares the sensor data.

Classify

The trained model predicts the movement category.

Respond

The screen and vibration motor communicate the result.
